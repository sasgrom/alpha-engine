## Steps

1. **Contradicted bars are unusable.** A screened jump whose second source disagrees by more than 1% (`other`) gets the
   same treatment as `single-source`: the bar's price level is removed, so **both** the return into it and the return
   out of it disappear. Show on two examples (VISN 2025-11-19, UHAL 2010-06-15) that no return touching the bad bar is
   left in `px.npz`.
2. **Whole-series check, not only jumps over 25%.** For every `schwab-yahoo` company with Stooq on the same dates,
   compare daily returns on all overlapping days. Report per company: overlapping days, days differing by > 2%, the
   share of such days. A company with more than 1% differing days (or more than 10 in any calendar year) is **listed**,
   with its cause found from the files (wrong share class, unit error, unadjusted split, stale or illiquid feed, or the
   other source is the wrong one), no guessing.
   - Listed spans are made unusable. Replacing them with Stooq is allowed only where Stooq agrees with a third source
     (fable1 / standard trIndex) on ≥ 20 overlapping days; otherwise unusable.
3. **Share class.** The panel's ticker must be the company's common stock. Fix HTZWW → HTZ and DJTWW → DJT, or leave
   the company unpriced where the common's history is not available. Scan all 1,405 tickers for warrants, units, rights
   and preferreds (suffixes W, WS, U, R, P and dotted or slashed classes) and report each with the chosen fix.
4. **Rebuild** as a new stamp (never overwrite `20261126T060023Z` or `20261127T052127Z`). Rerun the four gates after the
   exclusions, plus a fifth gate: **no in-window daily return in the panel differs from an available Stooq return by
   more than 2% unless that date is listed with its reason.** Report the remaining unpriced weight per quarter (this
   will rise; report how much).
5. **Hand check:** 30 random `other` rows, 20 days from the whole-series list, and both warrant fixes, against the raw
   source files; sign the review file as in K31.1.
