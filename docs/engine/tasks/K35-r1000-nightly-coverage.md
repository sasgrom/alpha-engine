# K35 — Keep the Russell 1000 companies current every night

Task for Kimi Code. **Owner's go of 2026-11-27** ("write k35"), after the owner's rule of 2026-11-25 for former members:
"unless they were delisted we should have them updated to current time". Written by Claude. Runs after K31.4. Kimi's
credits end 2026-11-30 16:00: build phase by phase, commit each phase when it passes, leave clean checkpoints; Claude
finishes whatever is cut.

## Why

K26–K28 and K31 gave the Russell 1000 companies outside our registry a full history up to 2026-11-27. The nightly jobs
cover only the registry (`worker/edgar-universe.json`, 502 companies) or the 50, so from now on these companies receive
no new filings, figures, prices or corporate actions, and every Russell test ages by a day each day.

## The universe (one file, rebuilt, never hand-edited)

- `worker/research-universe-r1000-current.json`, generated by a new `scripts/build-r1000-current-universe.mts`:
  1. Russell 1000 members of the newest N-PORT quarter (K26's membership files) that are **not** in
     `edgar-universe.json`;
  2. plus former members still listed (`edgar-universe-r1000-former-listed.json` rule: a current common-stock listing in
     SEC `company_tickers_exchange.json`, fetched through `secGet` and cached on D:);
  3. one row per company (CIK) with its common-stock listings (multiple classes kept as separate tickers), name, the
     reason it is in (member / former-listed), and the quarter it was last a member.
- Companies that join our registry later drop out automatically (no double collection). Companies that lose their listing
  drop out on the next build, with the reason recorded; their data stays.
- Output beside it: `D:/research/alpha-engine/index-membership/russell1000/current-universe/<stamp>.json` (source hashes,
  counts, joiners and leavers against the previous build). The worker file is rewritten only when the build passes its
  gates: CIK unique, every ticker resolves in the exchange list, the count is within ±5% of the previous build unless a
  new N-PORT quarter arrived (then the joiner/leaver list explains the difference).
- Weekly job `r1000-universe-weekly` (Sunday 05:20, before `news-company-index` at 05:50).

Expected size today: about 517 + 192 ≈ 709 companies.

## Phase 1 — SEC filings and figures (nightly)

- **Filings cache:** job `edgar-fetch-r1000`, `worker/edgar-fetch.mts --universe research-universe-r1000-current.json`,
  cron 02:40, depends on `edgar-fetch-daily`. The same cache, manifests and forms as the registry job.
- **Companyfacts:** job `facts-fetch-r1000`, the `sp500-facts-daily` rule (refetch a company's companyfacts only when a
  new financial/XBRL form or amendment arrived), cron 23:50, depends on `sp500-facts-daily`. K30's raw-first rule and the
  overlong-concept exclusion apply.
- **Release calendar:** the r1000 events (`D:/research/alpha-engine/engine/events/*-r1000.*`) get the nightly addendum
  that the registry calendar gets (`scripts/build-calendar-addendum.mts` pattern), as a new addendum file with its own
  manifest. The pinned calendar files are never rewritten.
- All SEC traffic goes through `secGet` and the shared K29 budget. No job raises `SEC_RPS`.

## Phase 2 — prices and corporate actions

- **Schwab:** job `schwab-fetch-r1000`, `worker/schwab-fetch.mts --price-series --universe
  research-universe-r1000-current.json --skip-preferences --root D:/research/alpha-engine/schwab-r1000`, cron 17:10 on
  weekdays only (after `schwab-fetch-sp500`; no 04:30 second run), 1,000 ms spacing as in K27. Market data only, as
  AGENTS.md requires. A new root: the existing archives are not touched.
- **Yahoo actions:** the companies join the split watch (`split-calendar`) and get the weekly corporate-actions pull that
  K27 part E used, written to a new dated folder; at most two retries per ticker, then the ticker goes to the run's
  todo file (owner's skip rule).
- **Panel refresh:** job `r1000-panel-weekly` (Sunday 06:30): rebuilds the Russell 1000 panel to a **new stamp** from the
  accepted K31.4 builder and the new raw data, runs gates 1–6 and the chain-id test, and moves a pointer
  `D:/research/alpha-engine/engine/r1000-panel/current.json` only when every gate passes. A failed build keeps the old
  pointer and is a job error. Stamps are never overwritten; retention is the owner's.

## Phase 3 — the market-wide sources that filter by company

- **Insiders:** `std-insiders-refresh` reads the SEC daily form indexes for all filers already; extend its issuer filter
  to the current universe, written as a separate r1000 output (new file family beside the standard; the loaded
  `std_insider_*` tables and the S&P standard stay as they are).
- **Short interest:** `finra-short-interest` keeps the rows of the universe's tickers in a separate r1000 file.

## Out of scope (do not build; listed so nobody assumes they run)

Standard fundamentals and guidance for these companies (no Russell standard exists yet); 13F; Congress; per-company
news feeds (the general feeds already resolve these names through the company index); Alpha Vantage (its free daily
limit is used up by the registry until about 2026-12-15); the app's company registry, desk pages and `alpha_engine`; the
research database; production.

## Rules

- New jobs go into `contracts/operations.ts` and by hand into `docs/OPERATIONS.md` (never regenerated). Each phase's jobs
  ship `defaultEnabled: false`; Claude enables them after accepting the phase. One scheduler: the scheduler restart that
  loads the new registry entries is announced in the mailbox first and done by Claude.
- Long outputs and logs on D:; nothing new on C: except source, tests and the universe file.
- Existing collectors get options, not forks: `--universe` and `--root` flags with tests, defaults unchanged so every
  existing job behaves byte-for-byte as before (prove with a dry run on the registry universe).
- Skip rule for every collector: a failing company or document gets two retries, goes to the run's todo file, and the
  run continues.
- First full night of each phase is a supervised run by hand, with timing, request counts and failures recorded here.
- AGENTS.md section 3.1 (D: interruptions) applies to every new job.

## Acceptance (per phase)

1. Tests for every new flag and the universe builder; `npm run check`, full tests, build and `git diff --check` pass.
2. Universe: count, joiners/leavers and gate results recorded; 10 companies checked by hand against N-PORT and the SEC
   exchange list.
3. A supervised night: per job, companies attempted / succeeded / in todo, SEC or Schwab requests, minutes, bytes on D:.
   No HTTP 429/403 from SEC; the registry jobs' runtimes unchanged within 10%.
4. Freshness check by data dates, not job status: for 20 random companies, the newest filing, companyfacts period,
   Schwab bar and (after phase 2) panel date equal what SEC and Schwab show.
5. Handoff section with commands, counts and hashes; the job list here updated with what was enabled.
