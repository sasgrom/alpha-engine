## Rule

A price segment belongs to the company (CIK) only inside **the company's own listed life**:

1. **Start:** the later of the first trading day of the ticker for this company and the company's own first periodic
   filing or registration (Form 10 / 10-12B effective, S-1/S-4 effective, first 10-Q/10-K) — from the stored SEC
   submissions (`engine/events/sec-submissions`). For a spin-off this is the when-issued/regular-way start of the new
   company; prices before it belong to another issuer and are cut.
2. **End:** a Form 25 or Form 15 ends the segment **only if it concerns the common stock** (check the filing's class
   of security / title in the stored index or document; a note, preferred or debt Form 25 does not). After a
   bankruptcy deregistration, a later re-listing of new common under the same CIK starts a **new segment** (rule 1),
   with the gap left empty — never joined by a return across it.
3. **Merger 8-K (item 2.01)** where the CIK is the **acquirer or the parent that continues** does not cut anything.
   It cuts only when the CIK is the one acquired (then its segment ends at the last regular trading day).
4. A ticker reused by a different company keeps the K31.1 treatment (cut), decided by rules 1–2, not by the first
   evidence date.
